# ADR-N. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-M | Rejected
- **Date:** DD Mon YYYY
- **Decided by:** name (the team lead decides if there is no agreement, Team Rules 2)
- **Related:** story IDs (for example E5-02), requirement IDs, hazard IDs

## Context

What is the problem? What constraint or fact forces a decision now? Link the evidence.

## Options considered

1. Option A: one line on what it is, its main benefit and main cost.
2. Option B: same.

## Decision

The option chosen and the reason, in two or three sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must change elsewhere (code, docs, tests, hazard log).
If this changes a baseline requirement, add an entry to the change log in `docs/planning/04_Team_Rules_and_Requirements.md` (Team Rules 12).

## Revisit trigger

The measurable condition that would make us look at this again (for example "p95 latency over 4 s on the reference phone").
