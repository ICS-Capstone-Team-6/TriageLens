# Ethics

The ethics pathway checklist and consent templates for human-subject work (TRD 12.3, Team Rules 8 and 9).

**Owner:** Kweku

## What goes here

- `irb-checklist.md`, `consent-template.md`, approval letters once they are received (with personal details removed)

## What must not go here

- Signed consent forms or participant names. Keep those offline, in the place the IRB approves.
- Interview recordings or notes that are not anonymized.
