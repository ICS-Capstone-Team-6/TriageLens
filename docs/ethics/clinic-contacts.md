## What to ask the first contact

1. Would you, or two or three colleagues, take part in a 30-minute conversation about how chest X-ray and eye screening and referral work today? (Only after the IRB has answered, see [ethics-pathway.md](ethics-pathway.md), section 2.)
2. Could you take part in a usability test in late November or December?
3. Is there anything about your facility's rules we should know before talking with staff? (If it is a Ghana Health Service facility, staff take part in their personal capacity; the study is not run inside the facility.)

Never ask for, accept or photograph patient images or records (Team Rules 8.1, 8.3).
