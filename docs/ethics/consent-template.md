## About this study

We are a team of four students at Ashesi University. We are building a phone app called TriageLens. It helps health workers sort chest X-ray and eye images into "refer", "not sure" or "low risk". It is a research project. It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose anyone.

We would like you to try the app and tell us what is easy and what is hard.

## What you will do

- Use the app on a phone for about [TODO: minutes] minutes.
- Do a few short tasks, such as taking a photo of a sample X-ray and reading the result.
- Fill in a short form with 10 questions about how easy the app was.
- Tell us what you think while you use it, if you are comfortable.

We use only sample images from public collections. **You will not see or share any real patient's information.**

## Your choices

- Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason.
- You can skip any question.
- Stopping or saying no will not affect you in any way.

## What we record

- Notes on what you did and said. We do not write your name in the notes.
- Your answers to the form.
- [ ] I agree to audio recording. (Optional. We will not record without this tick.)
- [ ] I agree to screen recording of the phone. (Optional. Your face will not be recorded.)

We keep notes on team storage only. We will report results in a way that does not identify you.

## Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond normal phone use. There is no direct benefit to you. Your feedback will help make the app clearer for health workers.
