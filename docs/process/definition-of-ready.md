# Definition of Ready

Copied from [Team Rules section 5.1](../planning/04_Team_Rules_and_Requirements.md). If this page and the Team Rules ever differ, the Team Rules win.

A story may start when:

- [ ] It has an owner and a supporter.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written and testable.
- [ ] Dependencies are named and either finished or scheduled before it.
- [ ] It is 4 points or fewer (split larger ones).
- [ ] Anything it needs (data, access, a device) is available.

Check this at backlog refinement (Wednesday of week 1) and before moving a card to **Ready**.
