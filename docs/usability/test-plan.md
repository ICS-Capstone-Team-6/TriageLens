# Usability test plan

Built from [TRD section 13.4](../planning/01_TRD.md), with targets from TRD 3.4 and Team Rules 10. Owner: Kweku (UX and HCI lead). Stories: E8-08 (round 1, Sprint 3), E8-10 (round 2, Sprint 4).

## Before any session

- [ ] Ethics approval or exemption received ([irb-checklist.md](../ethics/irb-checklist.md)).
- [ ] Consent form approved ([consent-template.md](../ethics/consent-template.md)).

## Participants (TRD 13.4)

At least 5 per round, from nurses, community health workers or nursing students. Written consent; no patient data.

## Tasks (TRD 13.4)

1. Install and open the app.
2. Screen one X-ray photo.
3. Retake a poor image.
4. Interpret an "Unsure" result.
5. Find a saved case.
6. Sync.

## Measures (TRD 13.4)

Task success, time to first result, errors, SUS, and what each person says the three zones mean.

## Targets (TRD 3.4)

| Metric | Target | Floor |
|--------|--------|-------|
| System Usability Scale (SUS) | at least 75 | at least 68 |
| Task success on first screening | at least 90% | at least 80% |
| Median time to first result | at most 90 s | at most 150 s |

Also checked: home to capture in 5 taps or fewer (NFR-USAB-01).

## Rounds (TRD 13.4)

| Round | Sprint | Build | Purpose |
|-------|--------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Sprint 3 | Before the guidance overlay | Baseline; top issues fixed before G3 |
| 2 | Sprint 4 | With the overlay | Compare retakes; SUS of at least 75 for G4 |

## Results

Record each round in `round-1-results.md` and `round-2-results.md` in this folder. Use anonymized participant codes (P1, P2, ...).
